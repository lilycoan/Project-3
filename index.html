      Creator

      Designed and built by Lily Coan as a prototype for a clothing brand aimed at US college students.

      Weather data

      Forecasts and place search come from Open-Meteo, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Values are in °F and mph, in the place’s local time, for today and the next 6 days.

      For “Use my location”, the place name comes from Nominatim. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the ODbL.

      Forecasts are less certain further ahead. NOAA notes a 7-day forecast is right about 80% of the time (NOAA NESDIS).

      How recommendations work

      WearCast looks at the hours you’ll be out: Day (8am–8pm) or Evening (5pm–11pm). For today it uses only the hours still to come. It uses the feels-like temperature, because humidity and sun change how heat feels (NWS Heat Index).

      Outfit, from the feels-like high

      Feels-like highOutfitBased on
      85°F and upHot: light, loose, light-colored clothesNWS heat guidance
      70–84°FWarm: tee or short sleeves, light trousers or a skirt—
      55–69°FMild: long sleeves or a light sweater—
      Below 55°FCold: warm layers and a jacketNWS cold safety

      If the feels-like low is under 70°F and in a cooler band than the high, the character carries a light layer tied at the waist.

      Privacy

      • Saved on this device: only your most recent place (its name and coordinates), in this browser’s local storage. Nothing else is saved: no dates, check marks or outfit picks. No cookies, analytics or trackers. To remove it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
      • Open-Meteo receives the place’s coordinates to send the forecast, and any city or ZIP you search for. It says its server logs, which can include coordinates and IP addresses, are deleted after 90 days and aren’t shared (Open-Meteo privacy).
      • “Use my location” asks your browser’s permission first. The position is rounded to about 1 km, then sent once to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) to name the place. The Foundation records requests, including IP address and browser, and says it doesn’t share them except with service providers or as required by law. Its policy doesn’t give a retention period for these records (OSMF privacy policy).
      • GitHub Pages, which hosts WearCast, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security (GitHub).
